WELLNESS & ACTIVE LIVING

About us

Wellness & Active Living is an independent editorial project publishing educational, practical information about everyday wellbeing, physical activity, sleep, mobility and healthy habits.

Our mission

We want to make general wellbeing information clearer and easier to use. Our goal is to help readers ask better questions, understand basic concepts and evaluate options thoughtfully.

How we develop content

We organize guides for readability, distinguish general information from opinion and aim to use reliable sources. We do not claim that a routine, product or habit produces identical results for everyone.

Independence and transparency

Some pages may contain affiliate links. If a reader makes a purchase through one of these links, the site may earn a commission at no extra cost to the buyer. Commercial relationships will be clearly disclosed and should not be confused with medical recommendations.
