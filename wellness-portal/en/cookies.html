Cookie policy

Current status: this initial version deliberately does not integrate analytics or personalized advertising tools. Hosting and external resources may have their own behaviour, which must be checked before launch.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website or provider may store in a browser to remember preferences, measure usage or enable features.

Third-party services

Before adding analytics, ads, embedded videos, forms or pixels, identify the cookies and technologies each provider uses and update this document.

Consent

Where applicable law requires it, use a consent management platform that allows visitors to accept, reject and configure non-essential technologies through equally accessible choices and withdraw consent.

Browser controls

Browsers allow users to review and delete cookies. Blocking them may affect some third-party website features.
