Affiliate disclosure

Transparency matters to us. Some articles may contain affiliate links to third-party products or services.

What does this mean?

Editorial independence

A commission does not mean a product is right for you. We aim to describe selection criteria, limitations and relevant features. We do not publish testimonials, tests or ratings that we cannot verify.

Clear identification

Commercial relationships should be disclosed clearly and close to the relevant link or recommendation, in addition to this general policy.

Programs
