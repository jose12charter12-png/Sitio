Editorial policy

Purpose

We publish general guides about wellbeing, physical activity, sleep, mobility and related products. Our aim is to inform, not diagnose or replace professionals.

Research and sources

Health articles should rely on trustworthy sources, prioritizing public institutions, universities, scientific reviews and relevant primary documentation. Specific claims should link to their sources within each article.

Authorship and review

Author and reviewer identities, experience and qualifications must be represented accurately. Credentials and clinical reviews must never be fabricated. Articles requiring specialist expertise should be reviewed by appropriately qualified professionals before publication.

Updates and corrections

We review content when relevant new information appears or errors are identified. Material corrections should be explained transparently.

Commercial relationships

Affiliate links and sponsorships will be clearly identified. Compensation must not dictate editorial conclusions or guarantee positive ratings.
